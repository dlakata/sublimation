# Read prompt — the shipped ask, given a shape

The shipped `act-analysis.md` says what to be an expert in and leaves the shape of the answer
to the model. Claude infers a shape; a 27B writes the genre. This variant changes nothing about
what is asked and everything about what the answer must contain — the same question with an
outline attached, so the structure axis can be measured apart from the detail axis.

---

## Prompt

Take the attached context (diffs from journals and notes, potentially physiology, finances, and
browsing history).

This upcoming week...

Tomorrow...

Today...

Emotionally, I...

As an expert in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy and psychometric profiling from a
clinical-psychology lens, provide stances to practice for the upcoming week. Keep it irreverent
where appropriate, and circumscribe what to hold loosely, how to approach what is on my mind,
and logistics or operations in the upcoming days such as summarizing any open loops. I'm open to
any psychoemotional reads, metaphors or challenges you may have as an expert in these areas.

Organise the read under these headings, in this order:

1. **What actually happened** — the week as events, not as themes. Name the people, the
   artifacts, the numbers, the days.
2. **Open loops** — every unfinished thing the notes reveal, each with the next physical action
   and the day to do it on.
3. **The stances** — the postures to practise this week, one heading each, each named as
   something to do rather than something to believe.
4. **What to hold loosely** — the beliefs the week is built on that are not load-bearing.
5. **The read** — the psychoemotional interpretation: a pattern the week did not announce about
   itself, supported by specific evidence from the notes.
6. **Challenges** — concrete assignments, each one falsifiable by the end of the week.
